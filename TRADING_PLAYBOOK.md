# $10,000 Swing Trading Execution Playbook

This playbook outlines the exact operational procedure for deploying and managing the **$10,000** swing trading portfolio starting Monday.

---

## 1. Operating Rules & Core Principles

1. **Signals Depend on the Most Recent Market Close**:
   - The US stock market operates Monday through Friday (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM EDT).
   - Signals for **Monday morning** are generated by the daily scanner at **4:30 PM EDT on Friday** using Friday's closing prices.
2. **Execution Timing**:
   - Entries occur at the **Monday Market Open (9:30 AM EDT)** using Market-on-Open (MOO) or market orders.
   - Bracket exit orders (Stop-Loss and Take-Profit) are placed immediately following fill confirmation.
3. **Holding Period**:
   - Trades are modeled on a **3 to 5 trading-day holding horizon**.
   - If neither the stop-loss nor take-profit is reached within 5 trading days, close the position at market or re-evaluate via the 4:30 PM daily scan.

---

## 2. Phase-by-Phase Execution Workflow

### Phase 1: Friday 4:30 PM EDT — Signal Generation

The automated daily scanner executes automatically at 4:30 PM EDT via macOS LaunchAgent and GitHub Actions.

1. **Check Alerts & Ticket**:
   - Review the automated email sent to `christopher@cmejo.com` or inspect the terminal ticket:
     ```bash
     cat LATEST_TICKET.txt
     ```
2. **Verify Macro Market Gate (QQQ)**:
   - Check line 10 of the ticket:
     - **BULLISH (Risk-On)**: Full risk-on trading approved (up to 5 concurrent positions, $P(\text{Up}) \ge 58\%$).
     - **BEARISH (Risk-Off)**: Remain **100% in CASH**. Do not execute new long orders.

### Phase 3: Monday 9:30 AM EDT — Broker Execution

Log into your brokerage platform (Interactive Brokers, Schwab, Fidelity, etc.):

1. **Enter the Buy Orders**:
   - Order Type: **Market-on-Open (MOO)** or **Market Order** right at 9:30 AM EDT.
   - Enter the exact share quantity listed on the ticket for each approved asset.
2. **Attach One-Cancels-Other (OCO) Bracket Orders**:
   - Immediately upon fill, enter a paired OCO bracket for each position:
     - **Stop-Loss (GTC)**: Set trigger price to the ticket's `GTC Stop-Loss`.
     - **Take-Profit (GTC Limit)**: Set limit price to the ticket's `GTC Take-Profit`.
   - *Advantage*: Guarantees downside risk is capped while ensuring gains are captured automatically without emotional interference.

---

### Phase 4: Monday–Friday — Portfolio Maintenance & Exits

1. **Intraday (Hands-Off)**:
   - Your active GTC bracket orders manage risk and profit-taking in real time. Intraday chart monitoring is unnecessary.
2. **Daily 4:30 PM EDT Scan**:
   - Run or inspect the daily scan after market close:
     ```bash
     ./run_daily.sh
     ```
   - When an open position exits via target or stop-loss:
     - Its capital returns to cash.
     - The next day's 4:30 PM scan automatically detects the newly opened cash slot and recommends the top-ranked candidate to fill it at the next open.
3. **Time-Based Exit Rule**:
   - If a stock remains inside the stop and target boundaries on trading day 5, exit at the market close or the following open to reallocate capital into fresh high-probability setups.
