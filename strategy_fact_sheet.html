1. Executive Thesis & Structural Alpha Edge Why the Strategy Works Across Bull, Choppy & Crash Regimes

A. Cross-Sectional ML Selection

Every day after market close, the engine scores ~515 liquid S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, and Russell 1000 stocks across 26 momentum, volatility, volume, and relative-strength features to estimate the calibrated probability of hitting +8.0% before -5.0% within 15 trading days.

B. Asymmetric 3-Tier Payoff Profile

Instead of binary exits, positions scale out in thirds: locking in base hits at +4.0% (moving stop to breakeven), harvesting core swing targets at +8.0%, and uncapping right-tail multi-week breakouts via a Chandelier Trailing Stop on the final 1/3 runner.

C. All-Weather Crisis Alpha Sleeve

Uninvested cash sweeps into SGOV (~5% Treasury yield). When the macro gate flips Bearish (SPY < 200-SMA or VIX > 28), long entries halt and idle capital rotates into inverse ETFs (SH/PSQ or SQQQ), turning crashes into positive compounding (+78.5% in 2022).

2. Five-Stage Quantitative Signal & Execution Architecture Zero-Lookahead Walk-Forward Pipeline

Stage 1 • Data
Universe Ingestion
Fetches 10-yr adjusted OHLCV for ~515 liquid equities + SPY, QQQ, & ^VIX. Filters out illiquid or sub-$10 names.
Stage 2 • Features
26-Factor Matrix
Computes multi-horizon ROC, RSI-14, MACD, Bollinger Width, ATR%, Keltner Squeeze, OBV Slope, CMF, and 20d/60d RS vs SPY.
Stage 3 • Regime
MSGARCH + Macro Gate
Classifies market into Bull, Defensive/Choppy, or Bear/Crisis using SPY 50/200-SMA, VIX, and MS-GARCH vol state.
Stage 4 • ML Scoring
ElasticNet (glmnet)
Walk-forward penalized logistic regression (α=0.5) predicts out-of-sample win probability P(+8% before -5%).
Stage 5 • Sizing
Vince Optimal f
Sizes positions up to 20% max equity, enforces 8% portfolio heat cap & 2/sector limit, and sweeps excess cash into SGOV/Hedge.

Why the Asymmetric Hybrid Gate Works: During intermediate pullbacks, raising the baseline gate to 65.0% filters out weak rebound trades, while allowing a 60.0% gate for institutional relative-strength leaders (RS20 ≥ +5%) captures early breakout winners.

26-Feature Cross-Sectional Factor Taxonomy

  • Price Momentum & Trend (7 features): 5d, 10d, 20d, and 60d Rate of Change (ROC); Price distance to 20-SMA, 50-SMA, and 200-SMA; ADX trend strength.
  • Volatility Compression & Tail Risk (6 features): 14-day Normalized ATR (ATR%), Bollinger Band Width & %B, Keltner Channel Squeeze ratio, 20d Realized Volatility vs. 60d Vol, Idiosyncratic skewness.
  • Microstructure & Volume Accumulation (5 features): 20-day Volume Z-Score, On-Balance Volume (OBV) linear slope, Chaikin Money Flow (20d CMF), Up/Down Volume Ratio, Intraday Close Location Value (CLV).
  • Mean-Reversion & Relative Strength (8 features): RSI-14, Stochastic %K/%D, MACD Histogram slope, Williams %R, 20-Day & 60-Day Relative Strength vs. SPY (RS20, RS60), and 60-day Rolling Beta.

3. Position Sizing, Portfolio Heat Controls & 3-Tier Trade Lifecycle Strict Downside Truncation with Right-Tail Convexity

Lifecycle Stage / Control Quantitative Rule / Trigger Execution Mechanics & Institutional Purpose
Entry & Position Sizing Limit Buy at Close • Ralph Vince Optimal f Sizes each position by calibrated edge, capped at 20.0% max account equity (~5 core slots). Blocks entry if stock gap exceeds +2.0% next morning.
Portfolio Heat & Sector Cap 8.0% Max Open Risk • Max 2 / Sector Sum of open risk Σ (Pricei − Stopi) × Sharesi across all holdings cannot exceed 8.0% of total equity. Maximum 2 concurrent stocks per GICS sector.
Initial Downside Stop Hard Stop at -5.0% (Entry × 0.95) Resting GTC stop-loss placed immediately upon entry. Also exits post-close if price breaks below the 50-day SMA on above-average volume.
Tier 1 Scale-Out (1/3 Shares) Limit Sell at +4.0% (Entry × 1.04) Banks an early "base hit" on 1/3 of shares and ratchets the stop-loss on the remaining 2/3 shares to Breakeven (Entry)—eliminating principal risk.
Tier 2 Scale-Out (1/3 Shares) Limit Sell at +8.0% (Entry × 1.08) Captures the primary swing target on the second 1/3 tranche and ratchets the stop-loss on the final 1/3 runner to +4.0% (Entry × 1.04).
Tier 3 Runner (Final 1/3) Chandelier Trail (Highmax − 2.5 ATR) Removes fixed profit ceilings on the final 1/3 tranche, trailing the highest high since entry minus 2.5 × ATR14 until trend exhaustion or Day 15 time stop.
Cash & Bear Hedge Overlay SGOV (Cash) + SH / PSQ / SQQQ (Bear) Idle cash earns ~5% annualized in SGOV. In confirmed Bear Regimes, deploys 30%–50% into 1x inverse (SH/PSQ) or 15%–25% into 3x inverse (SQQQ) with a trailing stop.

7. Turnkey Operator Guide: How Others Can Deploy & Run the Strategy 10-Minute Setup on macOS / Linux

Step 1: Environment & Initialization

  • Requirements: R (4.2+) with quantmod, TTR, glmnet, MSGARCH, data.table, jsonlite; Python (3.10+) with schwab-py / ib_insync, yfinance.
  • Clone Repository:
    git clone https://github.com/cmejo/R_SwingTrading.git
  • Set Starting Account Equity:
    python3 reset_evaluation.py --cash 25000
    Initializes portfolio.json and sets a clean benchmark baseline against SPY.

Step 2: Daily Execution (16:35 ET)

  • Mode A — Semi-Automated Ticket Workflow:
    1. Run Rscript daily_signal.R at 16:35 ET.
    2. Open LATEST_TICKET.txt for exact share counts, limit prices, 3-tier stop ratchets, and SGOV/SQQQ hedge adjustments.
    3. Log fills via python3 update_portfolio.py.
  • Mode B — Automated Broker API:
    Authenticate Schwab (execute_broker.py --broker=schwab --auth) or IBKR and schedule run_daily.sh --broker=schwab --live via cron at 35 16 * * 1-5.

Step 3: Monitoring & Governance

  • Live HTML Dashboard: Run python3 track_performance.py to generate performance_dashboard.html with real-time quotes, open positions, hedge sleeve weights, and cumulative alpha vs. SPY.
  • Statistical Review Cadence:
    • Daily: Verify cron & stop-loss integrity.
    • 30 Closed Trades (~3–4 Mos): Check win-rate (55%–62%) & payoff ratio (≥1.4x).
    • 6–12 Months: Evaluate Sharpe (≥1.5) & Max DD (≤28%).
Important Risk Disclosure: Historical backtest and out-of-sample walk-forward results include 10 bps round-trip slippage and realistic borrow/ETF expenses, but do not guarantee future performance. Leveraged inverse ETFs (SQQQ) experience path-dependent volatility decay if held without trailing stops; operators seeking lower volatility should select the 1x inverse sleeve (SH / PSQ) and the 65.0% Conservative Defensive Gate.
