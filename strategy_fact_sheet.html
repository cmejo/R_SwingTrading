1. Executive Thesis & Structural Alpha Edge Why the Strategy Works Across Bull, Choppy & Crash Regimes

A. 26-Factor Orthogonal ML Selection

Every day after close, the engine scores ~515 liquid U.S. equities across 26 orthogonalized predictors (collinear & negative-IC oscillators replaced; scale-invariant StdBeta = β·σX attribution) to estimate the calibrated probability of hitting +1.5R before -1.0R.

B. 15D / 20D Adaptive Runner Payoff

Positions scale out 50% at +1.5R (locking stop to breakeven & +0.85R ratchet) and trail the remaining runner via a 2.5× ATR Chandelier Stop. Confirmed mark-out leaders (TrendQuality ≥ 0.65 or ER20D ≥ 0.45, & RS20 ≥ +5%) extend from Day 15 to Day 20.

C. Squeeze-Guarded Dual-Bear Sleeve

Individual equities are traded strictly Long-Only. In Dual-Bear Regimes (QQQ < 50-lmMA & GLD Bearish), idle cash deploys into inverse ETFs (75% FNGD + 25% SQQQ, -10% stop) only when QQQ ≤ 20-lmMA; counter-trend bear squeezes automatically rotate to SGOV T-Bills.

2. Five-Stage Quantitative Signal & Execution Architecture Zero-Lookahead Purged Walk-Forward Pipeline

Stage 1 • Data
Universe Ingestion
Fetches adjusted OHLCV for ~515 liquid equities + SPY, QQQ, GLD, & ^VIX. Enforces $5M min daily dollar volume & 7-day earnings blackout.
Stage 2 • Features
26 Orthogonal Factors
Computes CMF_20D, ATR_Comp_5v20, VolumeExp_5v20, SortinoMom_20D, UpDownVolRatio_20D, LeftTailShock_20D, RS_60, & EfficiencyRatio_20D.
Stage 3 • Regime
MSGARCH + Macro Gate
Classifies market into Bull, Defensive/Choppy, or Dual-Bear using QQQ 50D/20D lmMA slope, GLD, VIX, and 2-state MS-GARCH probability.
Stage 4 • ML Scoring
ElasticNet + Platt
Purged 5-fold CV ElasticNet (α=0.5, 5-bar embargo) + Platt scaling predicts calibrated P(Up), applies Left-Tail Shock & ATR-Explosion guards, and ranks via Quality_Score.
Stage 5 • Sizing
Sortino-Adj Optimal f
Sizes top-3 candidates by Sortino/ER-adjusted inverse-vol (1.5x cap, 8% heat, Two-Tier -8%/-12% DD cushion) and deploys cash into SGOV or Dual-Bear Hedge.

7. Turnkey Operator Guide: How Others Can Deploy & Run the Strategy 10-Minute Setup on macOS / Linux

Step 1: Environment & Initialization

  • Requirements: R (4.2+) with quantmod, TTR, glmnet, MSGARCH, data.table, jsonlite; Python (3.10+) with schwab-py / ib_insync, yfinance.
  • Clone Repository:
    git clone https://github.com/cmejo/R_SwingTrading.git
  • Set Starting Account Equity:
    python3 reset_evaluation.py --cash 25000
    Initializes portfolio.json and sets a clean benchmark baseline against SPY.

Step 2: Daily Execution (16:35 ET)

  • Mode A — Semi-Automated Ticket Workflow:
    1. Run Rscript daily_signal.R at 16:35 ET.
    2. Open LATEST_TICKET.txt for exact share counts, limit prices, 3-tier stop ratchets, and SGOV/FNGD/SQQQ hedge adjustments.
    3. Log fills via python3 update_portfolio.py.
  • Mode B — Automated Broker API:
    Authenticate Schwab (execute_broker.py --broker=schwab --auth) or IBKR and schedule run_daily.sh --broker=schwab --live via cron at 35 16 * * 1-5.

Step 3: Monitoring & Governance

  • Live HTML Dashboard: Run python3 track_performance.py to generate performance_dashboard.html with real-time quotes, open positions, hedge sleeve weights, and cumulative alpha vs. SPY.
  • Statistical Review Cadence:
    • Daily: Verify cron & stop-loss integrity.
    • 30 Closed Trades (~3–4 Mos): Check win-rate (55%–62%) & payoff ratio (≥1.4x).
    • 6–12 Months: Evaluate Sharpe (≥1.5) & Max DD (≤28%).
Important Risk Disclosure: Historical backtest and out-of-sample walk-forward results include 10 bps round-trip slippage and realistic borrow/ETF expenses, but do not guarantee future performance. Leveraged inverse ETFs/ETNs (FNGD, SQQQ) experience path-dependent volatility decay if held without time/loss stops; the strategy enforces a strict 20D counter-trend squeeze guard, 15-day time stop, and -8.0% max-loss circuit into SGOV.
