Strategy Overview

The Triple-Engine Momentum Strategy is a rules-based, long-only U.S. equity strategy that seeks to compound faster than the Nasdaq-100 by combining leveraged index exposure with a concentrated sleeve of the strongest-trending stocks, while using trend and volatility rules to step aside in bear markets. Every decision is mechanical and made once a month from price data only; there is no discretionary stock picking or forecasting. When conditions deteriorate, capital rotates into gold (GLD) and borrowed margin is fully repaid.

How It Works — Three Engines

ENGINE 1 · CORE 30%

Leveraged index trend

  • Holds QLD (2× Nasdaq-100) while QQQ trades above its 200-day moving average.
  • Below the 200-day average, the core is sold and moved to gold.
  • Captures broad market upside with built-in bear-market exits.
ENGINE 2 · SATELLITE 70%

Relative-strength leaders

  • Ranks Nasdaq-100 stocks by a 50/50 blend of 6-month and 3-month return vs. QQQ.
  • Buys the top 3 (equal weight) that are above their own 200-day average.
  • −20% stop per stock; stop proceeds move into QLD until the next rebalance.
ENGINE 3 · RISK OVERLAY

Volatility & leverage control

  • Targets 22% volatility: when QQQ's 20-day volatility is higher, exposure shrinks and the excess goes to GLD.
  • 1.5× margin only in confirmed uptrends; 0% margin when QQQ < 200-day average.

Important disclosures. All results are hypothetical, back-tested simulations, not actual trading, and were prepared with the benefit of hindsight; no investor achieved these returns. Past or simulated performance does not guarantee future results. Methodology: January 2007 – October 6, 2026 (*2026 is year-to-date). Satellite stocks are drawn only from actual Nasdaq-100 constituents at each rebalance date (source: jmccarrell/n100tickers) to limit survivorship bias; however, ~67 former members that were later delisted (mostly acquired) lack free price history and are excluded, so a small residual bias remains. Earlier internal tests using a hand-picked list of today's winners overstated returns by ~20 percentage points per year; those figures are not shown. Prices are dividend-adjusted closes. Assumes 0.10% transaction cost per trade, margin interest at the 3-month T-bill rate + 1.75%, cash earning the T-bill rate, fractional shares, and no taxes, slippage beyond costs, or management fees. QLD history before its 2006 launch is not used. Risks: leverage and leveraged ETFs can produce losses exceeding those of the underlying index, margin calls, and decay in volatile markets; concentrated three-stock positions carry significant single-company risk; trend and volatility rules can whipsaw.
